Forget treadmills and dumbbells. At Woodsmen, you get fit by doing real, purposeful outdoor labour — hauling logs, swinging axes, clearing invasive pines, and carrying heavy loads through the bush.

What to Expect

SESSION TYPES

MON

The Fell

Felling day. Axe and hand-saw work to bring down wilding pines. Rotating technique drills, partner felling, and log processing. Raw, full-body power.

  • Strength
  • Skill
  • Technique
TUE

AxeFit

High-intensity axe-based conditioning. Chopping circuits, splitting rounds, overhead work, and timed efforts. Think CrossFit met a woodshed.

  • HIIT
  • Chopping
  • Splitting
THU

Firewood Carry

Loaded carries, log shuttles, sled drags, and team relays through uneven terrain. Build a back and legs that don't quit. Cardio meets raw strength.

  • Cardio
  • Strength
  • Teamwork
FRI

Social Planting

Plant native seedlings in cleared areas, build erosion barriers, do light trail work — then gather for kai and a yarn. Where community is forged.

  • Restoration
  • Social
  • Community

Community

FIND YOUR CREW

Woodsmen isn't just a gym — it's the people you grab a coffee with after hauling logs in the rain. It's the 60-year-old who pushes you harder than you thought possible. It's knowing your neighbours by name.

The Clubrooms — Earned, Not Given

The Woodsmen Clubrooms is your third space — not home, not work, but the place in between. A fire, a kettle, no screens, and people who get it. But you don't just walk in. You earn it.

To access the Clubrooms, you have to fell your own tree. It's a rite of passage. Your leaders will train you, guide you, and get you there — but until that pine hits the ground, the Clubrooms stays locked. Most people get there within their first few weeks. Some on day one. Either way, when you walk through that door, you know you earned it.

Out back, we run a native plant nursery — growing the very trees we plant on Fridays. From seed to soil, the full cycle happens here. Members help propagate, pot, and nurture native seedlings that go straight back into the cleared land.

FAQ

COMMON QUESTIONS

Do I need to be fit to start?

No. Every session is scaleable. We'll meet you where you're at and help you progress. Some of our best sessions come from complete beginners who bring nothing but willingness.

Is it safe? Do I need my own axe?

Safety is foundational. All equipment is provided and every session starts with a safety briefing. You'll learn proper technique before you touch a tool. We carry first aid and have trained session leaders.

What do I wear / bring?

Sturdy boots or trail shoes, clothes you can move and sweat in, and a water bottle. We provide gloves, tools, and everything else. Dress for the weather — we're outside rain or shine.

What about the wilding pines — are we actually making a difference?

Yes. We work in coordination with local councils and DOC. Wilding pine removal is real, funded conservation work. We're not playing at it — every session has measurable environmental impact on the land we work.

How much does it cost?

We're finalising our model. The goal is to keep it accessible. Sign up below and we'll share pricing details as we launch. Early members will get founding rates.
