The Model

NOT A FRANCHISE. A MOVEMENT.

Every Woodsmen Chapter is independently owned and operated. You run your sessions, set your pricing, hire your leaders, and build your community your way. We give you the brand, the programming, the training, and the network — you bring the grit.

01

Your Chapter, Your Rules

You own the operation. Choose your location (a pine-infested hillside, council land, private property with permission). Set your own schedule, pricing, and session types. We're not a franchise — we're a network of independently run chapters united by a shared mission.

02

Woodsmen Brand & Programming

Use the Woodsmen name, logo, and materials. Get access to Woodsmen Session Programming (WSP) — tested workout structures built around forestry, carries, and land restoration. Optional, not mandatory — you can programme your own sessions too.

03

Leader Training & Certification

All session leaders must hold a Woodsmen Leader Certificate. The course covers safety, axe/saw technique, session programming, scaling for all fitness levels, ecological restoration principles, and first aid. You can't lead until you're certified.

04

Community & Support Network

Join a network of Chapter Leaders across NZ and beyond. Share programming, swap ideas, solve problems together. Access the Woodsmen Playbook, marketing templates, and operational guides. You're independent, but never alone.

Investment

WHAT IT COSTS

One-Time $500 Application fee

Covers your application review, onboarding, and initial Playbook access. Non-refundable but credited toward your first year if approved.

Annual $1,500/yr Chapter license fee

Full brand license, programming access, network membership, and ongoing support. Payable monthly ($125/mo) or annually. That's less than most gym members pay.

Per Leader $300 Leader certification

Covers the Woodsmen Leader Certificate course. 2-day intensive covering safety, technique, programming, scaling, and ecological restoration. Recertification every 3 years.

No revenue share. No franchise fees. No royalties. You keep 100% of your membership revenue. The annual license is your only ongoing cost to Woodsmen HQ.

Requirements

WHAT YOU NEED

01

A Location with Wilding Pines

Access to land with invasive pines to clear — council land, DOC land with permission, or private property. Mobile/roaming chapters welcome as long as you have consistent access.

02

Certified Leader(s)

At least one Woodsmen Certified Leader must be present at every session. You (the Chapter Owner) must be certified. Additional leaders recommended as you grow.

03

Insurance

Public liability insurance covering outdoor fitness and forestry activities. We'll guide you to providers who understand what we do.

04

Basic Equipment

Hand saws, axes, gloves, first aid kit, and safety gear. No expensive gym equipment needed. A detailed equipment list is in the Playbook.

05

Council/Landowner Agreement

Written permission to operate on the land you're using. We provide agreement templates and can help you approach councils and DOC.

06

Commitment to the Mission

This isn't just a gym. You need to care about ecological restoration and genuine community building. If you're in it just for the money, this isn't for you.

Process

HOW TO OPEN A CHAPTER

1

Apply

Fill in the application below. Tell us about yourself, your location, your access to land, and why you want to lead a Woodsmen Chapter. Application fee: $500.

2

Interview & Approval

We'll have a conversation about your vision, assess your proposed location, and make sure we're aligned on values and mission. Not everyone gets approved — and that's intentional.

3

Get Certified

Complete the Woodsmen Leader Certificate — a 2-day intensive course. You'll learn safety protocols, axe/saw technique, session programming, scaling, and ecological restoration practices.

4

Set Up

Secure your land access, get insurance, acquire equipment, and build your launch plan. The Playbook walks you through every step. Your mentor is a call away.

5

Launch

Open your doors (or, more accurately, your treeline). Start running sessions, building your crew, and clearing pines. Welcome to the network.
