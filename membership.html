Weekly Programme

THE WOODSMEN WEEK

Every week follows a structured programme. Each day has a different focus so your body and mind get the variety they need — and the land gets consistent work.

Monday

THE FELL

Strength / Skill

Felling day. Axe and hand-saw work to bring down wilding pines. Rotating technique drills, partner felling, and log processing. Raw, full-body power.

  • Axe Work
  • Saw Work
  • Technique
Tuesday

AXEFIT

Conditioning / Power

High-intensity axe-based conditioning. Chopping circuits, splitting rounds, overhead work, and timed efforts. Think CrossFit met a woodshed.

  • HIIT
  • Chopping
  • Splitting
Wednesday

REST / ACTIVE RECOVERY

Recovery

No programmed session. Optional trail walk, mobility work, or just let your body recover. Good Woodsmen know when to rest the axe.

  • Recovery
  • Mobility
Thursday

FIREWOOD CARRY

Cardio / Strength

Loaded carries, log shuttles, sled drags, and team relays through uneven terrain. Build a back and legs that don't quit. The session that breaks and builds you.

  • Carries
  • Endurance
  • Teamwork
Friday

SOCIAL PLANTING

Community / Restoration

The week's reward. Plant native seedlings in cleared areas, build erosion barriers, do light trail work — then gather for kai and a yarn. This is where community is forged.

  • Planting
  • Social
  • Restoration
Saturday

OPEN SESSION

Mixed / Community

Rotating focus — could be a big fell, a team challenge, a skills workshop, or a community event. Check the Work of the Day each week.

  • Varies
  • All Levels

Session Structure

ANATOMY OF A SESSION

0–10 min

Briefing & Warm-Up

Safety briefing, tool check, and dynamic warm-up. Trail jog, bodyweight movements, and mobility specific to the day's work.

10–20 min

Skill / Technique

Focused skill work — axe technique, saw efficiency, carry mechanics, or planting method. Leaders demo, members practice. Progression built in.

20–60 min

The Work

The main session. Structured work with clear goals — trees to fell, logs to move, area to clear, seedlings to plant. Timed, measured, or team-based. Scaled to every level.

60–70 min

Finisher

Short, sharp effort. Max-distance carry, timed chop, or team challenge. Optional but encouraged. This is where you find your edge.

70–75 min

Cool-Down & Debrief

Stretch, breathe, debrief. Talk about what we accomplished, what's coming next week, and — on Fridays — stay for food and conversation.

Unlock The Clubrooms

Every membership includes access to the Woodsmen Clubrooms — our third space with a fire, a kettle, and a native plant nursery out back. But there's one catch: you have to fell your own tree first.

It's a rite of passage. Your leaders will train you, guide you, and get you ready. Most people earn their key within the first few weeks. When you walk through that door, you know you belong.

First Session

WHAT TO EXPECT

1

Show Up

Wear sturdy boots or trail shoes, clothes you can move and sweat in. Bring a water bottle. We provide everything else — gloves, tools, safety gear.

2

Get Briefed

Your leader walks you through safety, technique basics, and what's happening that session. No prior experience needed. Ask anything.

3

Work Hard

The session is scaled to your level. You'll work alongside people of all abilities. It'll be hard, but you'll never be left behind.

4

Belong

Stick around. Talk to people. Grab a coffee. This is where the community part happens. The people you sweat alongside become your crew.
